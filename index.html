From garden to table

Cook what you grow

Simple, seasonal recipes that make the most of Midwest harvests.

🥣

Roasted butternut squash soup

Roast cubed squash and onion at 425°F until tender, then blend with warm broth, sautéed garlic and a splash of cream. Season to taste.

Fall favorite
🍅

Garden tomato basil sauce

Simmer chopped ripe tomatoes with olive oil, garlic and onion until thick. Stir in fresh basil and season before serving.

Summer harvest
🥔

Herb-roasted garden potatoes

Toss cut potatoes with oil, rosemary, garlic and salt. Roast at 425°F until browned and tender, turning once.

Easy side dish
