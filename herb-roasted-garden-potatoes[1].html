Herb-Roasted Garden Potatoes

From your garden to your table

PREP TIME15 min
COOK TIME30–40 min
SERVINGS / YIELD4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb garden potatoes, scrubbed and cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 Tbsp chopped fresh rosemary (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 1 tsp chopped fresh thyme (or ½ tsp dried)
  • ¾ tsp salt, or to taste
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp chopped parsley (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment if desired.
  2. Scrub potatoes and cut into evenly sized pieces. Pat dry for better browning.
  3. Toss potatoes with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt and pepper.
  4. Spread in a single layer with space between pieces. Roast 30–40 minutes, turning once, until golden and fork-tender.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley if desired and serve hot.

❧ Garden-to-Table Tip

Use freshly harvested, firm potatoes for excellent texture. Avoid green potatoes and remove any green or sprouted potatoes from cooking.

For crisp edges, avoid crowding the baking sheet.
