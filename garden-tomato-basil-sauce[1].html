Garden Tomato Basil Sauce

From your garden to your table

PREP TIME15 min
COOK TIME35–45 min
SERVINGS / YIELDAbout 3 cups

Ingredients

  • 3 lb ripe garden tomatoes, cored and chopped
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)

Directions

  1. Wash, core and chop tomatoes; peel if preferred.
  2. Heat oil in a saucepan. Sauté onion 5–7 minutes, then garlic 30–60 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes, salt, pepper, oregano and optional pepper flakes. Simmer uncovered 30–40 minutes, stirring until thick.
  4. Stir in basil for the final 5 minutes. Taste; add sugar if needed.
  5. Blend for a smooth sauce or leave chunky. Serve with pasta or vegetables.

❧ Garden-to-Table Tip

Roma-type tomatoes are especially meaty. Freeze extra sauce in portions; this is not a tested shelf-stable canning recipe.

Use ripe, fully colored tomatoes for the richest sauce.
