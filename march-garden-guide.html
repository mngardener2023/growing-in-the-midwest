Monthly Garden Guide · USDA Zones 3–6

March: Start Your Spring Garden

Start tomatoes on schedule, care for seedlings, and plan cool-season outdoor sowing.

What to do this month

  • Start tomatoes about 6–8 weeks before transplanting
  • provide strong light
  • thin seedlings
  • inspect beds when thawed.

Midwest growing tip

Wait until soil is workable before outdoor planting; do not work wet soil.

Plan for your zone

Zones 3–6 have different frost dates, soil temperatures, and growing-season lengths. Check your local extension service and forecast before planting or transplanting.
